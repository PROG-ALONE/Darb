Q1) Reading Comprehension: (20 Marks)
A/ Read this text carefully then answer (5) of the questions that follow:   (10M.)

The computer is one of the most important inventions of the modern world. It has changed our lives in many ways. The first computers were very big and expensive. They filled a whole room and only a few people could use them. Today computers are much smaller and cheaper. Almost everyone can use a computer at home or at work. Computers help us to do many things quickly and easily. In medicine, computers are used to examine patients and to help doctors to find out what is wrong with them. Scientists use computers to do difficult calculations. Pilots use computers to know directions and to fly safely. Even children use computers to learn and to play. In short, the computer is one of the greatest inventions that has ever changed our life.
